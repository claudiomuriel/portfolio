AH, LISBOA!

Eletrico, Pessoa and the light

In 2025 it was the second time I went to Lisbon with a camera. The first time, in 2010, although getting some very good photographs, I had not enough skills and vision to make a photo essay, and most of all, I lacked the idea of making photo series. This time, I focused in yellows, the eletrico and the dusk in the atlantic ocean.
